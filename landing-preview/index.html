The Street & Counter Order Flow

How InstaDine Works Every Day

Customers order from their phone and pay you at the counter with Cash, Card, or Contactless QR. No customer credit cards required online.

01
Table QR

1. Scan & Order

Customer scans the table or counter QR. Your visual menu loads in under 1 second without downloading an app.

02
Phone Docket

2. Get Order Number

Customer chooses items, picks Dine-In or Takeaway, and receives Order #104: "Pay at counter."

03
Cashier POS

3. Pay & Confirm

Customer pays cash/card at counter. Staff taps "Confirm & Paid". 1 credit is deducted.

Only Confirmed Orders Cost Credits!
04
Kitchen Print

4. Food Ready!

Kitchen cooks order. Customer's screen rings with voice notification: READY FOR PICKUP.

🛡️
Zero Risk: Abandoned & Fake Orders Cost You Nothing
If a customer enters an order and leaves without paying, it expires or gets rejected. You are never billed a single credit.
